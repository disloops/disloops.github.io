This page compiles the nutrition and fitness stuff that I'm doing at any given time. Disclaimer: These are my personal preferences and not advice! This is just a repository of information for my own reference.

## Supplement Routine

I don't like plain water. Instead I fill a [half gallon glass jug](https://www.amazon.com/NiceBottles-Glass-Half-Gallon-Pack/dp/B07C7TSQ1M) with water and add a cup of [pure cranberry juice](https://lakewoodorganic.com/products/lakewood-organic-pure-cranberry-juice-32-ounce-pack-of-6). It helps to wash down vitamins and I try to get through two each day. I think it's important to drink cold water as soon as you're awake before consuming anything else. I also drink coffee mixed with this [Collagen and MCT Oil](https://www.amazon.com/Perfect-Keto-Chocolate-Protein-Powder/dp/B072KFZ9TQ). Collagen supports the structure of your hair, skin, muscles, bones and connective tissues.

Update: Although I am now trying this instead: [Noble All-in-One Protein: Chocolate](https://nobleorigins.com/products/noble-all-in-one-protein-chocolate) - "Grass-fed beef protein isolate, collagen, colostrum, and a unique organ blend."

I take three supplements when I exercise:

- [Transparent Labs BULK Pre-Workout](https://www.transparentlabs.com/products/bulk-preworkout) – Citrulline and beta-alanine for muscle output, L-theanine and theobromine for smooth energy. No dye or artificial sweeteners.
- [Transparent Labs Creatine HMB](https://www.transparentlabs.com/products/creatine-hmb) – Creatine is probably the single most [proven](https://jissn.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/s12970-017-0173-z) performance-enhancing supplement that exists.
- [Naked Whey Double Chocolate](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B0CGXZCWC1) - Uses real sugar instead of a sweetener. I mix this with whole milk, ice, banana and strawberries in a little blender cup.

I take the following supplements after dinner each day because I want to have a full stomach first. I've intentionally purchased serving sizes that allow me to take one pill of each supplement (although the directions on some bottles may recommend more than that). In alphabetical order:

- [Angry Supplements Apple Cider Vinegar + Beet Root](https://angrysupps.com/products/apple-cider-vinegar-with-beet-root) – Apple cider vinegar supports digestive health and beet root is a natural nitric oxide booster that improves blood flow and exercise performance.
- [BioGaia Gastrus Probiotic](https://www.biogaia.com/products/gastrus) – Supports healthy digestion. This strain - **Lactobacillus reuteri** - is also [believed](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3879365/) to support testosterone.
- [FenuTrax Fenugreek Extract](https://fenutrax.com/) – Some [studies](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/ptr.6627) suggest that fenugreek supports healthy testosterone levels and muscle growth through its active compound protodioscin.
- [Gaia Herbs Hawthorn Supreme](https://www.gaiaherbs.com/products/hawthorn-supreme) – Lowers blood pressure and the [Chinese](https://www.dailymail.co.uk/femail/article-11641107/Chinese-medicine-expert-reveals-NATURAL-alternative-weight-loss-drug-Ozempic.html) seem to like it.

There's also less biotin, which is a DHT blocker.
